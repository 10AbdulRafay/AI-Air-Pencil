Core Features

Everything happens in the air.

AI Air Pencil combines computer vision, gesture recognition and a modern drawing interface into one interactive experience.

✦

Hand Tracking

Real-time hand landmark detection allows the application to understand the position and movement of your fingers.

✎

Air Drawing

Move your index finger through the camera view and create digital strokes without touching a physical surface.

◉

Gesture Controls

Switch between drawing, color selection, brush sizing, erasing and saving through simple hand gestures.

↔

Dynamic Brush

Pinch your fingers to dynamically control the brush size and create different stroke styles.

↶

Undo & Redo

Built-in drawing history makes it easy to experiment and safely reverse or restore previous canvas states.

↓

Instant Saving

Save completed drawings and automatically view them in your personal drawing gallery.

How It Works

Four simple steps.

The interaction is designed around natural hand movements so the interface stays simple while the computer vision does the complex work.

01

Show Your Hand

The webcam captures your hand and the AI hand-landmark model detects its key points.

02

Make a Gesture

Different finger combinations are interpreted as drawing, selecting, erasing or saving actions.

03

Draw in the Air

Your index fingertip becomes the virtual pencil cursor and creates strokes on the digital canvas.

04

Save Your Art

When you're finished, save the drawing and continue creating your next digital artwork.
